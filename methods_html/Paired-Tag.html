Paired-Tag

Paired-Tag combines antibody-directed protein A-Tn5 tagmentation with reverse transcription and split-pool barcoding to measure histone modifications and RNA in the same nuclei. This page describes the original 2021 plate-based chemistry (12 initial barcodes and two 96-well ligation rounds).

Unlike Paired-seq, the initial DNA tagmentation is antibody-directed. The original design pairs one targeted histone modification with RNA per nucleus; the five histone marks in the publication were profiled in separate experiments. Later 384-well and droplet-based variants are outside this page's scope.

Step-by-step library generation

Diagrams show the barcode-bearing strand in its 5' to 3' direction. Bracketed segments are schematic, not literal DNA; lengths are not to scale. The complementary strand is omitted until the final library overview.

(1) Anneal adapters and prepare barcoded nuclei reactions

Anneal each DNA_BC01 oligo with pMENTs and load protein A-Tn5. Prepare matched oligo-dT and random-hexamer RT primers for each of the 12 initial tubes. Separately anneal BC02 with Linker-R02, and BC03 (R04) with Linker-R03. The linkers bridge the ligation junctions.

First ligation oligo (BC02):

5'- GTGCGAACTCAGACC[7-bp BC02]ATCCACGTGCTTGAG -3'
Second ligation oligo (BC03):

5'- CAGACGTGTGCTCTTCCGATCT[10-bp UMI][7-bp BC03][0-3-bp phase block]GTGGCCGATGTTTCG -3'

(2) Antibody-directed tagmentation adds BC01 to genomic DNA

Incubate nuclei with the histone-mark antibody, bind barcoded protein A-Tn5 and activate tagmentation. Each transferred adapter contains the common ligation handle, a DNA-specific A, BC01, a NotI site and the Tn5 mosaic end. Only one tagged end is drawn.

DNA after targeted tagmentation:

5'- AGGCCAGAGCATTCGA[4-bp BC01]GCGGCCGCAGATGTGTATAAGAGACAG[genomic DNA] -3'

(3) Reverse transcription adds the matching BC01 to cDNA

Reverse-transcribe RNA inside the same nuclei using the matched barcoded RT primer mix. Both primer types carry a common handle, an RNA-specific T, the same BC01 and an SbfI site. The random hexamers are priming bases, not the UMI.

RNA-derived first-strand cDNA:

5'- AGGCCAGAGCATTCGT[4-bp BC01]CCTGCAGG[T16VN or N6][cDNA] -3'

(4) Pool and split nuclei into 96 wells: first ligation (BC02)

Ligate the pre-annealed BC02 adapters to the 5' ends of both DNA and cDNA. Block unused first-round linkers with Blocker-R02, then pool and wash the nuclei.

DNA:

5'- GTGCGAACTCAGACC[7-bp BC02]ATCCACGTGCTTGAGAGGCCAGAGCATTCGA[4-bp BC01]GCGGCCGCAGATGTGTATAAGAGACAG[genomic DNA] -3'
cDNA:

5'- GTGCGAACTCAGACC[7-bp BC02]ATCCACGTGCTTGAGAGGCCAGAGCATTCGT[4-bp BC01]CCTGCAGG[T16VN or N6][cDNA] -3'

(5) Split into 96 wells again: second ligation (BC03 and UMI)

Ligate BC03 adapters, adding the Read 2-side handle, a 10-base UMI, BC03 and the phase block to both modalities. Quench with Quencher-R03 and EDTA, pool and wash. The original barcode space is 12 × 96 × 96 = 110,592 combinations before subdivision into sublibraries; this is not a collision-free cell capacity.

(6) Divide nuclei into sublibraries, lyse, C-tail and pre-amplify

Divide the barcoded nuclei into sublibraries before lysis. Denature recovered nucleic acids, then use terminal deoxynucleotidyl transferase (TdT) and dCTP to add 3' poly(C) tails. Anchor-FokI-GH primes second-strand synthesis from these tails and introduces the FokI-containing anchor. PA-F and PA-R then amplify the barcoded products.

Sequencing layout

The authors' protocol specifies at least 50 cycles for Read 1 and 100 cycles for Read 2, using standard Illumina sequencing primers. Its example is R1 50 + I1 7 + R2 100. Supplementary Table 1 encodes a 6-base i7 in P7XX, whereas the protocol example allocates 7 index cycles; choose the index mask to match the actual ordered primers and sample sheet.

Read 2: identical barcode layout for DNA and RNA

Read 1 supplies genomic or transcript sequence for alignment. Read 2 is primarily a barcode read, so this is not a conventional paired-end genomic-fragment layout. Barcode sequences in the CSV are already in Read 2 orientation.

Use linker-aware extraction rather than one fixed set of offsets for all wells. The authors' reachtools combine2 handles the original 7-base ligation barcodes; combine3 is for the later 8-base design. The cell key must include sublibrary + BC03 + BC02 + BC01, with matched sublibrary labels for DNA and RNA. Keep the UMI separate from cell identity.

I1 reads the reverse complement of the i7 sequence in P7XX. If collecting I2 from the RNA library, account for the instrument-dependent i5 orientation described in the Illumina overview; the original DNA library has no i5 index.
